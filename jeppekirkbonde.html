E.g. tell an Uber driver to drive over there, or a delivery guy to deliver a box over there. Many people already work for an AI, basically. Their job is to open an app or an email and do what it says. 2) The AI or the organised swarm of AIs should run across many servers, so you can't just shut off one server. Backup instances must regularly be activated, server security in place including backup power sources, etc. As AI continues becoming more capable, people and organisations feel pressure and temptation to let AI run critical infrastructure: Military drones, self-serving cars, monitoring via street cameras, and the likes. 3) With abilities of self-driving, analysing what its cameras sees, operating many different types of drones and vehicles, AI can increasingly operate a part of, then most of, and ultimately all of an industrial site. AI will e.g. start to operate a mining site mostly and eventually fully autonomously. If a mining vehicle breaks down, it sends an autonomous vehicle to repair it. Deciding when to stop the crusher for inspections, etc. It operates security at the mine autonomously, opening / closing the gate without a human in the loop, and driving metal concentrate away in autonomous trucks.
